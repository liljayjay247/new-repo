I guess he was about twelve or thirteen and knew how to ride the bike but I had never been on anything except my push bike. He was giving kids rides on the back up and down the long path and it then came to my turn. I was a little nervous but I climbed on to the back of the bike, no helmet! and off we went. At first just pootling around the lower lawn, but then up the long concrete path. The feeling was amazing. Long-distance rides often redefine a person's life. A notable historical example is Elspeth Beard, who in the early 1980s packed her 1974 BMW R60/6 and embarked on a two-year, solo journey around the world, becoming one of the first British women to accomplish the feat. For historical travel, the legendary South American journey taken by Che Guevara in 1951—which was later chronicled in The Motorcycle Diaries—remains a classic tale of self-discovery and cultural awakening.
